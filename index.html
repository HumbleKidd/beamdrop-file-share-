Frequently Asked Questions
How does Beamos transfer files without servers?
Beamos utilizes pure WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels. Your files travel straight device-to-device through an encrypted browser pipeline without touching or storing on any cloud backend.
How does the auto-deletion work?
Every payload carries an independent countdown timer (15 minutes by default or your custom setting). Once zeroed, the item and its memory blob unmount completely from the screen.
Can I preview HTML, images, and video right here?
Yes! Clicking view opens a clean in-app modal right on this page—no new tabs or page refreshes. Images render in original resolution, media plays inline, and HTML runs in safe sandboxes.
Can I add files before scanning or pairing?
Yes. Drop your files into the portal right away. They stay in the staging queue and automatically blast over the millisecond your devices link up.
