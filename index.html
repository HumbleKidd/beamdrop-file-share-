Frequently Asked Questions
How does the QR code auto-open the website?
The QR code encodes your full current web URL with your PIN attached (?sync=123456). Scanning it with any standard phone camera opens Beamos in their browser and establishes the peer link instantly.
Can I preview my own files before connecting?
Yes! Every photo, video, music track, or HTML code you drop is fully interactive in your visual galleries immediately without needing an active peer connection or backend server.
How does the auto-deletion work?
Every payload carries an independent countdown timer (15 minutes by default or your custom setting). Once zeroed, the item and its memory blob unmount completely from the screen.
How does Beamos transfer files without servers?
Beamos utilizes pure WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels. Your files travel straight device-to-device through an encrypted browser pipeline without touching or storing on any cloud backend.
